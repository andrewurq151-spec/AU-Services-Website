What we do

Services built on precision, compliance, and clarity.

From annual financial statements to day-to-day bookkeeping, every engagement follows the same Golden Standard: accurate, on time, and easy to understand.

Now offering

Body corporates & homeowners associations

We compile annual financial statements for sectional title schemes and HOAs, together with draft budgets and levy schedules. This gives trustees clear, AGM-ready numbers, delivered with the accuracy and fast turnaround trustees have come to rely on. For schemes that require a statutory audit, we work alongside specialist body corporate & HOA audit partners.

  • Annual financial statements compiled for AGMs
  • Draft budget preparation
  • Levy schedules
  • Known for our accuracy and fast turnaround
  • Audit coordination through specialist body corporate & HOA audit partners
02

Annual financial statements

Compiled per ISRS 4410 (Revised) and IFRS, so your statements stand up to scrutiny from banks, SARS, and stakeholders alike. Every set includes a full Operating & Financial Overview Report as standard, not an optional extra.

  • Compilation engagements per ISRS 4410 (Revised)
  • IFRS / IFRS for SMEs compliant presentation
  • Operating & Financial Overview Report included
03

Tax & compliance

We manage your full SARS relationship, covering income tax, provisional tax, VAT, and PAYE, so deadlines are never a scramble. A full tax computation is included with every set of annual financial statements.

  • Individual and company income tax returns
  • Provisional tax calculations and submissions
  • VAT registration, returns, and reconciliations
  • PAYE, UIF and SDL administration
  • SARS correspondence, disputes and registrations
  • CIPC and statutory filing support
04

Bookkeeping

Monthly bookkeeping and bank reconciliations keep your records year-end ready, with no last-minute catch-up. Fees are based on transaction volume, so pricing scales fairly with the size of your business.

  • Monthly bookkeeping and bank reconciliations
  • Management accounts and reporting
  • Transaction-based pricing, scaled to your business
05

Payroll

Payroll is administered accurately and on time, every pay cycle, including the statutory submissions that come with it.

  • Payroll processing and payslips
  • EMP201 submissions and EMP501 annual reconciliation, including IRP5 employee tax certificates
  • Compensation Fund (COID) return submissions
06

Business advisory

Beyond compliance, we help you plan ahead: financial planning, cash flow forecasting, and practical insight so decisions are made with real numbers, not guesswork.

  • Financial planning and budgeting
  • Cash flow forecasting
  • Business structuring and registration guidance
  • Ad-hoc management reporting and insight
