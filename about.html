About us

Refined by principle, not just by process.

AU Accounting Services was built around a simple idea: like gold, financial clarity is refined, not assumed. Every engagement follows that same discipline.

Our story

AU Services (Pty) Ltd trades as AU Accounting Services, providing accounting, payroll, taxation, and statutory compliance services to corporate and individual clients across South Africa. Our name draws on the periodic symbol for gold — Au — because that's how we think about the work: raw financial data, refined through process and precision into something clear, dependable, and valuable.

We call it The Golden Standard: a commitment to holding every engagement, large or small, to the same level of care.

Our mission

To raise the standard of business in South Africa through industry-leading expertise, practical experience and client education:
empowering businesses to make informed decisions, grow with confidence and achieve lasting success.

Our vision

We envision a South Africa where every business has the confidence and clarity to grow: equipped with the right expertise, or backed by a trusted partner who can provide it. When businesses have clarity, they make sound decisions that build lasting success.
